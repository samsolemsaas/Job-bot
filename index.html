Every conversation

How it works. Every morning Hallway Track reads public conversations on Hacker News, Information Security Stack Exchange, infosec.exchange, Google News and Reddit. Each company lens decides which buyer problems it can speak to and how credibly, then scores every conversation on fit, who is asking, whether anyone has answered and how fresh it is.
Privacy. Public posts only. It never joins or reads private groups, it doesn't store usernames, and every excerpt links to the original. Rooms are recommended from public evidence so the team can request access the normal way.
